01

What is Agentic AI Consulting?

It is the work that happens before and alongside engineering: choosing the right problems, the right architecture and the right level of autonomy, so AI investment is tied to business outcomes.

02

AI readiness assessment

A structured review of data availability, systems, skills, security posture and organizational readiness, giving a clear view of strengths, gaps and prerequisites.

  • Data and systems review
  • Security and compliance review
  • Skills and operating-model review
03

Business process discovery

We map how work really flows, including inputs, decisions, handoffs and exceptions, to find the steps where agents can assist or act.

04

AI opportunity mapping

Candidate use cases are assessed on business value, feasibility, risk and effort, producing a prioritised shortlist instead of a long wish list.

05

Feasibility analysis

We test the riskiest assumptions early, such as data quality, accuracy requirements and integration access, so investment decisions rest on evidence.

06

Agentic architecture strategy

Recommendations on single-agent versus multi-agent designs, model options, knowledge strategy, tool boundaries and human oversight.

07

AI governance and security

Policies and controls for permissions, approvals, audit logging, data handling and evaluation, aligned to your regulatory context.

08

Implementation roadmap

A phased plan with milestones, dependencies, ownership and success criteria, ready to execute with your team or with us.

09

Consulting engagement model
