Customer Support AI

Agents that help support teams answer faster and route work correctly.

What agents can do

  • Customer enquiry handling
  • Support ticket classification
  • Knowledge retrieval
  • Escalation workflows

How it works

A support agent reads an incoming enquiry, classifies it, retrieves the relevant knowledge-base articles and drafts a reply. Anything sensitive or low-confidence is escalated to a person with the context attached.

Scope, accuracy and outcomes depend on your data, systems and process. A discovery phase confirms what is feasible.
